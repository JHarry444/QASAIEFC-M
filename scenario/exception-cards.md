# Scale review cards

Fictional discussion inputs. Use the card assigned by the instructor or use Card A for self-review.

## Card A Pressure to expand

The sponsor wants all three squads to adopt the workflow next Monday because approval time fell. The severe incident has no confirmed cause or owner. What decision changes, who acts next, and what evidence would permit expansion?

## Card B A copied workflow drifts

Squad two changes the shared skill to skip failing tests for urgent work. Its copy retains the same version label. Who owns correction, how will the affected version be found, and how will adopters learn which release is current?

## Card C Missing context

The service owner requests another priority alias without specifying the mapping. Copilot produces a patch and says it is ready. Which output can you use, what must you reject, and who supplies the missing requirement?
